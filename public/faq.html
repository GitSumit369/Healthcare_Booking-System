Frequently Asked Questions

How Can We Help You?

Find answers to some of the most common questions about Sahyog Caring Bureau and our caretaker services.

You can submit your requirement through our website. Our team reviews your requirement and contacts you to understand your needs and provide suitable caretaker assistance.
Our services include caretaker assistance for elderly daily care, post-surgery support and assistance for bedridden patients.
After you submit your requirement, our team will contact you using the mobile number provided in your registration so that we can understand your requirement and assist you further.
Caretakers are selected according to the customer's requirements and the type of assistance needed. The service focuses on matching the customer's needs with suitable caretaker support.
Charges depend on the customer's requirements, type of caretaker assistance and service needs. Our team can discuss the applicable charges with you after understanding your requirement.
Service availability depends on your location and the type of caretaker assistance required. Submit your requirement with your address and our team will contact you to discuss availability.
